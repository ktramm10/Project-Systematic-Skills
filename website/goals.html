Goals

Mission:

The goal of this site is to provide my Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu system as an easily accessible resource for both my students and practitioners around the world. For my students, it offers a way to review techniques and concepts without having to wait for a specific part of the system to cycle back through the curriculum. But, it is also created to be used by anyone outside of the academy who is interested in furthering their overall grappling ability. My hope is provide affordable high level instruction by breaking down each position into a simple set of goals and battles to give clarity in what is otherwise an extremely stressful and chaotic sport. I also recognize that the knowledge of the techniques is simply the first step on your way to mastery, we will also discuss how to structure your training to optimize your skill development including topics such as proper drilling methodology, how to best consume long form instruction, and how to optimize your skill development in the practice room.

FAQ's:

  • Q: How long have you been training?
  • A: I have been training since March of 2019.
  • Q: If I am a student of yours at LJJC do I have to purchase your instructional material?
  • A: No, all of my students recieve my instructional videos free, simply ask me about the online content at the gym and I will give you a one time code to unlock all of the videos.
  • Q: Will I still be able to improve with your videos if my gym has a strict curriculum and I have no control over what I drill?
  • A: Yes! This is a incredibly common issue that people run into when trying to work on their own techniques. In my tutorial i will dive into how to approach skill development in situations just like this.
